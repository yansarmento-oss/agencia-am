**How to apply:** When Yan works on this, think SYSTEM (agent + Notion integration + SOP for the team) not one-off copy. Use Notion MCP. Route to copywriters who master authority + short-form conversion (Bencivenga, Schwartz, Koe, Halbert hooks). First 3s hook is non-negotiable.
